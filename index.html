About Me

I live in Sydney, Australia, where I moved having grown up in the nearby Blue Mountains.

For work I’ve historically been a Data or Analytics Engineer. I have a bunch of experience building pipelines, data models, that sort of thing. Also a lot of experience working implementing analytics. I’d generally consider myself a bit of a generalist though, and I love working with everything from low-level system engineering to web development to DevOps, learning as much as possible about tech and different domains! I’m currently looking for opportunities, if you’re looking to hire!

I enjoy solving problems, the more puzzling the better, so like diversity in my roles where I get exposed to lots of moving parts.

At home I like playing with more niche technologies than I get to work with at work. Right now I’m excited about:

  • Zig
  • FreeBSD
  • Rust

I previously helped organise the Sydney chapters of Data & Analytics Wednesday and MeasureCamp, which are both free and you should come attend!

I like other stuff too (in no particular order): both kinds of games (board and video), music, politics, cooking, bushwalking/hiking/camping, gardening, beer, wine, spirits, cats, combinations of these things.
